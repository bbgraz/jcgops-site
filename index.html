Got Questions?

We have straight answers

What does JCG Ops actually do?

Our customers are small home-services contractors, so even if you're a company of one, we can help you scale. We build small bespoke systems that optimize your business processes or help you build the processes. We make sure incoming leads get captured and answered, estimates go out and get followed up, and customers are properly communicated with.

How much does it cost?

Pricing starts at $99 per month. The exact price depends on what we design or build for your business, and we tell you the number in writing before any work starts. There are no long-term contracts.

Do we have to change the software we already use?

No. We design into your current business workflow and the tools you already pay for, whether that is ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, a spreadsheet or a notebook by the phone.

Do we need a software developer on staff?

No. There is no software developer dependency. We build it, we train your team to run it, and you keep running it without us.

Who is this for?

Small home-services businesses with 1 to 12 people: HVAC, plumbing, pool service, electrical, roofing or other small companies in Miami-Dade and Broward County, including Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Coral Springs, Miramar, Pompano Beach, Davie, Plantation and Sunrise.
