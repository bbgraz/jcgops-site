Straight answers

Questions before we start.

Do I need to replace my current software?

Usually no. JCG Ops starts by understanding what you already use and where manual work still exists around it. If a current system already solves the problem well, we should use it.

What happens in the free Workflow Triage?

We spend 15 minutes on one operational process you dislike. The goal is to determine whether there is a meaningful problem worth investigating. It is qualification, not a free consulting engagement.

What is the $1,000 Operations Audit?

The Audit maps the workflow, identifies the highest-value operational gaps, and gives you a written implementation plan and fixed price. Under the current test offer, the Audit fee is credited toward the implementation if you proceed within 30 days.

What does implementation cost?

Most first automation installs are currently scoped in the $5,000 to $7,500 range after the Operations Audit. The exact number depends on the workflow, systems, integrations, and scope.

How long does implementation take?

The target is to get the first high-value automation creating value within roughly 2 to 4 weeks where the technical scope supports it. You receive the actual timeline in writing before implementation starts.

Is this a long-term contract?

No forced long-term optimization contract. Ongoing JCG Ops Optimization is month to month and has to keep earning its place.

Is JCG Ops another CRM or field-service platform?

No. JCG Ops works around the tools you already use whenever practical. We focus on workflows that still require people to chase, copy, remember, or reconnect the process manually.

Do you only work with HVAC companies?

No. JCG Ops is starting with HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and related owner-led service businesses because many of the operational patterns are similar.
